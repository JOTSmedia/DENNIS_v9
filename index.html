The adjustment

What actually changes when a segment is corrected

A rotated, side-bent vertebra shares one address with the joint, the disc, and the nerve root. The correction is a direction of drive that lets that segment seat. In the study above, the gold ring is the contact. Story

He was the patient before he was the physician.

The work started with his own first adjustment — a change in a long illness, and no interest afterward in doing anything else.

The practice that followed is subluxation-based, and deliberately wider than the joint. Clinical nutrition. Diet protocols that are not generic. Exercise built in short bursts. Biochemical analysis. And what he calls chemical subluxation: the chemistry that keeps a segment from holding.

Private practice in New Jersey and New York followed. Personal injury and major medical care. People who had already been everywhere. Functional medicine in the same room as the adjustment. Enough years in the room to know what a spine does under a real week, not only under a textbook.

Doctor of Chiropractic, Life University, Marietta, 1996. Bachelor of Arts, Rutgers University, Newark, 1991. Licensed in New Jersey since 1996 and New York since 2011. The office now is in Rutherford. New patients are being accepted.

Practice

Six ways of looking at the same person.

The adjustment is the center. The rest of the visit asks why the segment would not stay.

Chiropractic correction

Segmental adjusting, including cranial and blocking work. Nonsurgical care for disc injury, stenosis, and sciatica, with Cox Technic flexion-distraction when the disc is the finding.

Functional medicine

Blood chemistry, clinical nutrition, thyroid and metabolic patterns. Diet that is specific. The chemical side of a segment that will not hold.

Functional neurology

Postgraduate applied neurology. How the nervous system recruits a segment, a gait, or a headache. The adjustment is a neurological event.

Neurofeedback

Training regulatory patterns so the correction has a quieter system to live in. Used with the hands-on work, not instead of it.

Applied kinesiology

Muscle testing in the Goodheart tradition, from private study, to locate which segment and which system is asking to be corrected.

Trigenics

Certified neurological muscle work, particularly for shoulders and knees that will not hold a structural correction on their own.
